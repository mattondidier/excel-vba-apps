# ELITE: Training Centre Management in Excel VBA

🇫🇷 [Version française](README.fr.md)

**From enrolment to dashboard: courses, students, trainers, payments and treasury of a training centre, in a single Excel application driven by VBA.**

Developed from 2020 to October 2021 for ELITE, a training and services centre.

![ELITE demo](assets/elite-demo.gif)

▶ **Demo of the first version (2020):** [watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ftuCQo1PRnY) (in French)

## The problem

A training centre needs to manage its programmes, enrol students, track payments and receivables, pay its trainers and steer its treasury. ELITE brings all of this into one tool, with a dashboard that shows the state of the centre at a glance.

## Features

**Dashboard**
- 20 indicators: certified and non-certified courses, completed and ongoing seminars, enrolled students and averages per course, trainers
- Outstanding receivables and payables, monthly receipts, expenses and result, payroll, daily and cumulative cash balance, annual course performance

**Courses and students**
- Course and programme creation, course activation and deactivation
- Session planning, enrolment and attendance tracking
- Student database with contact details, automated enrolment
- Payment and receivables tracking

**Performance tracking**
- Student assessment and progress reports
- Feedback tools for trainers and students

**Daily operations**
- Enrolment ledger and miscellaneous operations ledger
- Teacher payments and cash control

**Settings**
- Treasury accounts and cash movement reasons
- Courses, students, employees, trainers and other third parties

**Analysis**
- 15 reports: course-by-student cross analysis, daily treasury and performance per cashier, overall treasury position
- Receipts, expenses and result by period, pending payments
- History of enrolments, miscellaneous operations, trainer payments and cash flows
- Charts, reports exportable to PDF and Excel

**Communication and help**
- Message templates, e-mail campaigns and bulk SMS
- User management, built-in technical sheet and user manual

## Under the hood

| Item | Detail |
|------|--------|

| Interface | Home dashboard, side menus, a UserForm for each module |
| Data | Settings, ledgers and history stored in the workbook |
